Your Turn — What can I play?

  • You lead? Any card (Trumper R1: not Trump unless Open).
  • Following: Same suit as led if you have one.
  • Can’t follow (Closed): Fold a card.
    • Cut: Trump-suit, face-down (guess).
    • Minus: non-Trump, face-down (concede).
  • Can’t follow (Open): Any card, face-up.
  • Trumper never folds in-hand trumps (§T-4).

Bidding Quick Reference

  • Min open: 160 (4-card), 220 (8-card)
  • Increments: 10 (<200), 5 (≥200)
  • Below-200/250: first speech only, no undercut partner
  • Termination: all 4 spoke + 3 consecutive passes
  • Partner: 4-card only, dealer’s right or opposite, first speech, no return
  • Pass-on: no bid (4-card) or hand < 25 (8-card)
  • Redeal: 4 cards < 15 points (priority or partnered partner)

Caps Outcomes

  • Correct, before R7: win + 1 stone bonus
  • Correct, R7+: normal win
  • Late Caps: loss + 1 stone (game flipped)
  • Wrong/Early: 5 stone penalty
  • External Caps: mirrors above, framed against betting team’s loss
  • PCC: stake decided on all-8 only; no Caps bonus

Pass-on / Void Triggers

  • 4-card betting ends with no bid
  • 8-card hand < 25 points (first speech)
  • Spoilt Trumps (opp. holds zero Trump from deal)
  • Absolute Hand (goodwill or accepted claim)

Redeal: same dealer reshuffles. Pass-on: deal moves anticlockwise.
